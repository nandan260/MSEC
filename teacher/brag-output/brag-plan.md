# Teacher Desk — Space to teach

## Intent

Create a cinematic Blender film combining the user's college logo, the actual
editable dashboard model and the modeled teaching desk. The user delegated
creative direction. All work remains local; do not push to GitHub.

## Format and identity

- 22 seconds, 24 fps, landscape 1920×1080, H.264 MP4 with stereo AAC.
- Dark plum stage, lavender rim lights, warm ivory surfaces and sage UI.
- Preserve the supplied MS Engineering College logo exactly as a texture.
- Typeface: locally available Noto Sans; restrained, readable scene titles.
- Original synthesized atmospheric score and soft transition accents; no narration.
- Real application data shown is fictional demo data, not institutional records.

## Storyboard

1. **0–4s — Identity.** A beveled logo plaque turns into the light. Lavender
   rings trace its silhouette. Logo settles large and clear for over two seconds.
2. **4–8s — Space to teach.** Camera glides past the actual 3D desk, books,
   laptop and plant. Headline: “Made for teaching.” Short subtitle establishes
   classes, attendance and progress.
3. **8–14s — Dashboard reveal.** Full dashboard floats into view. Panels settle
   in a staggered sequence and weekly teaching bars rise from their baselines.
   Hold the complete interface after the motion settles.
4. **14–18s — Focus.** Camera moves closer across the actual chart, attendance,
   learning-health and task panels. Title: “Everything, in focus.”
5. **18–22s — Brand finale.** Logo and miniature dashboard share the stage.
   Closing line: “More space to teach.” Hold the final composition to the end.

## Execution and checks

- Build one editable Blender timeline with named stage collections and cameras.
- Retain source scripts, source logo, `.blend`, soundtrack, poster and MP4.
- Use GPU rendering if available. Inspect stills for every scene and transitions
  before rendering the full sequence.
- Verify codec, duration, audio, first/last frames and scene readability.
- Open the finished scene in Blender and provide a playable MP4.
